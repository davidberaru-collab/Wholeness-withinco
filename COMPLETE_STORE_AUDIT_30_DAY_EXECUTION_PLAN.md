# Wholeness Within Co. — Complete Store Audit + 30-Day Execution Plan

Date: May 13, 2026
Audit inputs: provided screenshots (Homepage, Collections, Blog article template), existing strategy docs in this repo.

## 1) STORE PROBLEMS FOUND

### Brand & Positioning
- Homepage first impression is promotion-led rather than authority-led (sale-first hero).
- Premium brand story is not clear within first viewport.
- Messaging does not immediately anchor to sensitive-skin transformation and “inside out” healing philosophy.

### UX/UI + Conversion
- Primary navigation is too generic for guided shopping (missing concern-based paths).
- Collection labels have professionalism issues (grammar consistency, naming architecture).
- Product cards are image/title/price heavy but benefit/trust-light.
- In-grid trust and proof elements (rating/reviews, badges, routine step) are limited.

### Merchandising
- Product assortment presentation is not merchandised by outcome (barrier support, hydration, sensitivity).
- No obvious bundle ladder or ritual-based order-builder flow in visible screens.

### Lead Capture & Lifecycle
- Email capture exists but value proposition is weak (generic “join email list” copy).
- Missing visible lead magnet mechanics (guide/quiz/ritual plan).

### SEO & Content
- Blog visuals are strong, but conversion path from content to product likely underdeveloped.
- Content likely under-internal-linked to specific PDP and routine bundles.

### Trust & Authority
- Trust claims are present but not deeply proof-stacked (clinical-style claims, sourcing proof, customer outcomes).
- Lack of layered trust modules near product discovery moments.

## 2) HIGH-PRIORITY FIXES (ROI-ordered)

### Priority 1 (Immediate): Homepage authority rebuild
- Replace discount-first hero with brand-led hero and concern-based CTA split.
- Add trust proof bar directly below hero (review volume, guarantee, shipping promise).
- Add “Shop by Concern” module before general collections.

### Priority 2: Collection conversion upgrade
- Rename/restructure collections by outcome.
- Add benefit microcopy and trust badges on product cards.
- Add quick-add and routine step tags.

### Priority 3: PDP trust stack
- Add benefit-first bullets above fold.
- Add ingredient/sourcing accordions + safety notes.
- Add UGC/testimonial blocks near ATC.

### Priority 4: Lead magnet funnel
- Replace passive newsletter with high-value lead asset and 7-day nurture sequence.
