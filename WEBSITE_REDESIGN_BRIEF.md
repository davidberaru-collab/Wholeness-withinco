# Wholeness Within Co. — Website Redesign Recommendation (Professional Luxury Upgrade)

Date: May 13, 2026
Scope source: visual audit from provided storefront screenshots (`home`, `collections/all`, and `blog` templates).

## Executive Summary
Your store has a strong product foundation and clean palette, but it currently reads as **early-stage template storefront** rather than a premium luxury skincare brand.

The biggest issue is not product quality—it is **perceived brand authority at first glance**. In luxury skincare, perceived authority directly affects conversion rate, AOV, and willingness to subscribe/repeat purchase.

## What is currently hurting a “professional luxury” feel

### 1) Homepage hero is promotional but not brand-defining
- Current hero prioritizes sale messaging (“20% OFF STOREWIDE”) over premium positioning and transformation story.
- Missing a clear founder/value statement above the fold.
- CTA hierarchy is weak (one generic “Shop now” button).

**Impact:** attracts discount shoppers, weakens premium pricing power.

### 2) Navigation and information architecture are too generic
- Top nav is basic (Home/Shop/Contact/About/Blogs), with no guided shopping by concern.
- No immediate pathways for “Sensitive Skin”, “Mother’s Routine”, “Barrier Repair”, etc.

**Impact:** users browse but don’t self-identify quickly, reducing conversion intent.

### 3) Collection card naming/visual consistency feels unpolished
- Label inconsistency (“Womans Collection”, “Mens Collection” grammar issues).
- Product and model imagery styles vary heavily (lighting/background/aesthetic inconsistency).
- Pinterest save badge and mixed overlays create clutter.

**Impact:** visual inconsistency lowers trust and luxury perception.

### 4) Product grid is not optimized for premium ecommerce conversion
- Cards emphasize image + title + price but lack quick trust cues (skin concern, scent, key benefit).
- Product naming structure is inconsistent in length and clarity.
- No visible review snippets/badges in listing context.

**Impact:** weak scannability and lower click-through to PDP.

### 5) Typography hierarchy and spacing are not “editorial-luxury” yet
- Large serif headings are elegant, but supporting rhythm (subheads, body, whitespace cadence) lacks intentional structure.
- Some sections feel too sparse without storytelling anchors; others feel dense.

**Impact:** design feels template-driven rather than art-directed.

### 6) Blog template is visually strong but commercially under-leveraged
- Article hero is attractive, but likely missing in-article conversion modules (product callouts, lead capture, ingredient references).

**Impact:** content may generate reads but under-converts to revenue and email capture.

## Recommended Redesign Direction (Professional Luxury System)

## 1) Brand Positioning Layer (above-the-fold)
Replace discount-first hero with a **brand-first hero**:
- Headline: “Healing skincare for sensitive skin—rooted in nature.”
- Subline: “Grass-fed tallow, botanical actives, and ritual-based wellness.”
- Primary CTA: “Shop by Skin Concern”
- Secondary CTA: “Take the 60-second Ritual Quiz”
- Add founder trust bar: “Small-batch • Non-toxic • Sensitive-skin safe • Made with integrity”

## 2) Premium Homepage Architecture
Proposed section order:
1. Hero (brand story + 2 CTAs)
2. Trust proof bar (reviews count, return policy, shipping promise)
3. Shop by concern cards (Dryness, Sensitivity, Barrier Support, Glow)
4. Best-sellers with quick benefit badges
5. “Why Tallow” education block (simple, evidence-led)
6. Before/after or customer story carousel
7. Founder note + mission (“Because healing starts from the inside out.”)
8. Email capture with value lead magnet

## 3) Collection Experience Upgrade
- Rename and reframe collections with customer outcomes:
  - “Sensitive Skin Essentials”
  - “Hydration & Barrier Repair”
  - “Daily Ritual Staples”
  - “Men’s Wellness Grooming”
- Add sticky filters by concern, scent, skin type, and routine step.
- Add badge system on product cards:
  - “Best Seller”
  - “Sensitive-Skin Safe”
  - “Unscented Option”
  - “New Formula”

## 4) Product Card Standardization
Each card should include:
- Product image (consistent style)
- Short title (60 characters target)
- One-line benefit (“Calms redness + supports barrier”)
- Price + comparison/subscribe hint
- Star rating + review count
- Quick-add button (mobile friendly)

## 5) Visual Direction System (to look professionally branded)
- Palette: warm ivory, oat, amber, espresso text, muted botanical green accent.
- Photography: one consistent lighting style (soft directional natural light).
- Packaging framing: similar crop ratios and backgrounds.
- Typography: luxury serif for headlines + clean sans for utility text.
- Consistent spacing scale (8pt system) and section rhythm.

## 6) Blog-to-Revenue Conversion System
On all blog templates:
- Mid-article product callout cards
- Sticky “Shop this routine” rail (desktop)
- 2 CTA blocks: “Shop routine” + “Get the free skin reset guide”
- End-of-article quiz CTA + FAQ links
